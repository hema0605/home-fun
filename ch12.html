Home-Fun game

Day and Night

Listen and tap

For parents

Tap the sun, the moon, the star and the lamp when the phone asks.

Sit with your child and play together. It takes about 5 minutes. If the phone does not speak, read the question aloud. Then put the phone away.

Well done!

For parents: now put the phone away

Look at the moon together before bed. Keep the same steps every night: wash, story, sleep.
