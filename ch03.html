Home-Fun game

My Body

Listen and tap

For parents

Tap the part of the body that the phone names. Then touch it on your own body.

Sit with your child and play together. It takes about 5 minutes. If the phone does not speak, read the question aloud. Then put the phone away.

Well done!

For parents: now put the phone away

At bath time, name each part as you wash it. Let your child wash the tummy and the feet.
